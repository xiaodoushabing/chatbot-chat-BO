V1 · Ledger

Feel: dense, flat, hairline borders, small radius. "What changed" is an inline redline (struck-out = live today, underlined = after release), like tracked changes in a contract.

For the demo audience: the fastest read of what actually changed. Very "instrument". It may feel severe to business users.

V2 · Studio

Feel: your current app's own language: soft cards, large radius, generous spacing. "What changed" is shown as stacked before/after cards, with "After release" marked by a crimson edge.

For the demo audience: the closest match to the screens they already approved. It's calm and familiar, and it takes up the most vertical space.

V3 · Device

Feel: the customer's view. The pre-live bot sits in a phone frame, as customers will see it in the app. "What changed" is shown as today's reply → the reply after release, drawn as chat bubbles.

For the demo audience: the most tangible ("this is what your customer gets"). The phone frame makes the chat column narrower.
